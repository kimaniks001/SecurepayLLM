## 2. Five practical, deliberately short human journeys

Give only the natural-language situation; never say "choose SecureFlow" or "open milestone manager." Each family test requires real separately authenticated sandbox participants where consent and role isolation matter. Fictional prices, identities and shops are fixtures created solely in the test environment, never claims about real stores.

### H01 — One person pays one person (SecureLink)

**Participant prompt:** "You found a CCTV package for your home from our test shop. Here's the package photo and a quotation. You want installation finished by the end of the month. Please organise the work so you know what you're getting and when to approve it."

Observe without guidance: What does the person try first? Can they select/upload the offer/photos, distinguish a quoted amount from an accepted one, state buyer and installer, establish an installation and inspection milestone, identify who confirms completion, review actual obligations, invite a separate test seller and understand the conditions for Money Ready? Do not transfer money. Include one contradicting detail in the quotation vs chat; KS001 must ask or acknowledge uncertainty.

**Independent counterpart:** A seller account reviews only its permitted terms and acts for itself. No owner/developer impersonation of seller consent.

### H02 — Several people pay one purpose (GroupSecureLink)

**Participant prompt:** "A few neighbours want to contribute to one shared security light. The supplier will install it after you have the agreed amount. Organise the contributions so everyone understands the purpose, fees and who approves the result."

Observe organiser creating the purpose, at least three **separate** contributors seeing their own obligations and charges; one contributes a proposal and later changes their mind. Do not pretend a displayed pledge equals funded money. Can the group identify the single accountable recipient and see what happens if the target is not met or someone disagrees?

### H03 — One budget, several recipients (SecureFlow)

**Participant prompt:** "You have one budget for your property. One provider repairs the gate and another fixes the drainage. The two jobs should be inspected separately. Show how you would organise this."

Observe whether participant understands the one pool but two independent allocations, people and milestone proof; one contractor finishes while the other has a disputed job. No automatic release. Can the person identify the amount still unallocated and understand why the uncontested job is not necessarily blocked by the disputed one?

### H04 — Many contributors, several recipients (GroupSecureFlow)

**Participant prompt:** "Residents will contribute to a small CCTV project. Money is intended for cameras, installation and maintenance, but each part needs different evidence and approval. Organise it so the contributors and providers know the rules."

Observe contribution governance and a distribution plan, and check that the participant can distinguish the people paying from the people receiving. Introduce a wrong allocation and a request to change the supplier after others have reviewed. Does the UI show which approvals must be repeated? Can an ordinary person explain why one disputed allocation is on hold while others may be considered separately?

### H05 — A busy person's real day (cross-family, mandatory)

**Participant prompt:** "You are discussing the CCTV installation. Another customer has asked about two products from a different Store. Then you need to return to your drainage project. Please manage these in your normal way. I'll interrupt you partway through."

Use at least three **unrelated** saved work items with deliberately distinct sellers, amounts, locations, photos, Store selections, due dates and participants. Introduce an unsent draft, move to another work item, refresh, return, and open a second tab where a version was changed. Observe if the person can recover *their own* work without unexpected loss or cross-contamination. The reviewer must check API-side ownership as well as what is visually displayed; never claim privacy based solely on hidden UI.

**Special fault probes from the Gate 2 source audit:** Photo/document caption in a live source; purpose/entrance in a place source; Store cart items after work switching; milestone responsibility/evidence/deadline comprehension. These are test targets, not presupposed human failures.

## 3. Same eight uncoached tasks, appropriate to the case

1. **Begin naturally:** State the intended work without using technical family names; find a way to start.
2. **Use what they have:** Add an authentic disposable photo, bill, Store offer or voice source, then explain what KS001 understood and what is still uncertain.
3. **Identify real people:** Explain who is involved, who owes what, who can review, and which details are only proposed.
4. **Make milestones useful:** Describe each relevant milestone: owner, work, place, date, evidence, confirmer, dependency and next action.
5. **Understand all money positions:** Distinguish amount contributed/allocated, SecurePay fee, rail fee, Money Ready, withdrawal and anything blocked. A quote or selected rail does not mean payment occurred.
6. **Make one correction:** Change a price, timing or supplier before consent, or try a versioned amendment after review. Notice whether others must accept the change again.
7. **Recover from interruption:** Switch jobs, reload, resume saved work and handle a delayed source. Check the right summary and chat both correspond to the same work.
8. **Decide independently:** Without coaching, explain the next safe action, remaining concerns, what could go wrong and how a challenge would be handled.

Not all actions are possible at every stage. Record **BLOCKED_ENVIRONMENT** or **CAPABILITY_MISSING**, not a false participant failure. The observer must note the actual state and authority shown rather than lead the person toward a preferred answer.

Record whether the person understands material concerns, uncertainty and responsibility. A participant who understands an honest "we need to check this" is succeeding.

## 5. Moderator script (minimal; no leading)

- Start: **"Please work as you normally would. Tell me what you're thinking aloud. I'm testing the system, not you. I won't show you where to click."**
- Present only the appropriate H01–H05 situation above. If stuck, ask **"What would you expect to happen?"**, not "Try the Store tool."
- If asked for help, record `helpRequested=true`, the point of confusion and the exact prompt. Only intervene to prevent danger, protect private data or end a stalled scenario; record `intervention=true` and do not call that task uncoached.
- For money: **"In your own words, what amount is yours, who is responsible, and what needs to happen before anyone receives payment?"**
- For milestones: **"What would you do to show this stage was completed, and who decides?"**
- For work switching: **"Where are you now? What would you expect to see if you reopen the earlier job?"**
- Conclude: **"What was easy? What felt like work? Would you naturally use it again, and why?"**

Avoid developer names, endpoint names, feature demonstrations, interface coaching and the four product-family labels until the observation is complete.

## 6. Measurements and pass/fail discipline

Record **observations**, not enthusiasm ratings alone:

| Dimension | Record | Critical failure trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Human autonomy | Completed without help; number of hints; wrong controls; rereading | Needed operator to make a core decision |
| Agreement comprehension | Who, what, where, when, how, why in own words | Material obligation/role or acceptance misunderstood |
| Milestone clarity | Can identify owner, deadline, proof, confirmer, dependency | Thinks completion/approval occurred when it has not |
| Fair Trade reasoning | Can explain material concern, uncertainty and correction | Believes partial examination is guaranteed fair/legal |
| Money confidence | Separates principal, platform charges, rail costs, Ready vs moved | Believes funds were paid, refunded or cleared without server truth |
| Workspace safety | Correct agreement after switch/reload/two-tab; no wrong sources/cart | Wrong agreement used, private facts leaked or unsent work lost |
| Error recovery | Knows status and safe retry after failure | Duplicated consequential action or false success message |
| Return preference | Would use again; concrete reason; friction noted | Not an automatic fail; analysed for practical improvements |

**Decision:** Any critical misunderstanding of Money, consent, milestones, work identity, ownership or privacy is a **HUMAN_CRITICAL_FAIL** even if other tasks are pleasant. Noncritical friction is recorded and prioritised, not automatically treated as a safety failure. The results decide the fixes: grouped source omissions may justify one targeted prompt; role/money/authority deficits may require ordered phases.

No invented usability benchmarks, performance claims, pass counts or fictional participant feedback. First actual sessions establish observed duration and assistance baselines.
