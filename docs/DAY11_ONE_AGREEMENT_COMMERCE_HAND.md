# Day 11 — One Agreement Commerce hand across Store, Mini and Ask KS001
Date: 12 November 2026
Status: isolated owner-review candidate. Not production-certified.

## Product doctrine

One commercial system, three contexts. The buyer sees an appealing familiar shop. The
seller sees offers AND commitments that need execution. The compact Mini is the SAME
agreement/financial handoff, never a replacement storefront. Ask KS001 is contextual
help, not a second Store, order ledger, chatbot or payment authority.

**Agree · Deliver · Grow** is the shared expression.

1. **Shop (buyer):** verified published offers, familiar browsing, beautiful media,
   price/availability from records, search, cart, optional Ask KS001. Selecting goods
   does NOT create an order, binding agreement, guarantee of stock or payment.
2. **My Store (seller):** own offer controls; agreement obligations; genuine next action;
   milestone/evidence; approved customer changes; commitments and deadlines; optional
   supporting services/sub-agreements for fulfilment. Funding/settlement is separate,
   verified by the selected authorised provider.
3. **Ask KS001:** concise answers grounded in authorised store/offer/agreement facts.
   Optional chat or human-readable next-action pills. Never reveal seller margins or
   another buyer's agreement. Opening the Store never resets an existing chat.
4. **SecurePay Mini:** preserve the approved one-screen compact checkout. Receive only
   authorised agreement context; show one verified source, appropriate available
   payment options, next step and safe return to the originating host. No forced chat.
   Never infer PAID or agreement acceptance from browser simulation.
5. **Community Saver:** opt-in reciprocal intelligence, with distinct consent for
   categories, audience, agreement-specific disclosures and viewer receipts. Published
   capacity is not a booking. My People, chosen community or wider eligible market
   are audience controls. A reminder is not confirmed availability.
6. **Sensitive providers:** accommodation, storage and transport require independent
   vetting. An ordinary KSNumber or Store never authorises these categories.
7. **Execution chain:** a parent Agreement may be supported by transport, supplies,
   storage and subcontract agreements. Parents remain responsible to their buyers
   unless the original agreement is explicitly revised by authorised participants.
   Supporting agreements have independent acceptance and financial authority.
8. **Community growth:** one-off spare capacity and recurring agreements both work.
   Routes, cargo compatibility, timing, price, security, loss/liability and added
   handling must be verified before a match becomes a proposed agreement.

## Non-negotiable consistency

- Preserve the Living Workspace three panels: left Tools/Store browsing, middle KS001
  chat or explicitly expanded Store item, right glance-first summary and editable cart.
  No fourth panel, duplicate chat/controller or duplicate cart.
- Preserve mini.html and existing approved Mini funding simulation; do not restore
  archived Phase 5 host demo/navigation experiments.
- Keep seller shop branding customisable; underlying agreement-status language,
  privacy and status truth must be consistent regardless of entry point.
- Do not silently join two diverged development histories. Day 9 Store review and
  10 November approved Mini each get an isolated, parallel Day 11 branch.
- Actual Store and Money API authority is required before any status is payable,
  funded, reserved, matched, verified or settled.

## Gate 1 changes in this branch

- A shared domain/copy contract in experienceContract.ts with separate execution and
  financial state representations, strict permissioned sharing and mutual receipts.
- Seller Store retains its existing layout and real offer/fulfilment controllers.
  It now carries a concise Agreement Commerce identity and a collapsed Community
  Saver consent experience. The consent controls are **preview only**, default OFF;
  no network calls, persistence or data sharing. They are not a subscription.
- Ask KS001 Store contextual guidance uses the same copy contract.
- Approved compact Mini's existing sourced Store-offer review uses the same
  offer-only wording where that component exists on this branch.
- Focused regression assertions; no broad tests or migrations.

## Release safety and cross-branch reconciliation

Two isolated branches of the same frontend repo exist because the Day 9 Store and
approved Phase 4 Mini histories have diverged by hundreds of commits. These are
review candidates, NOT equivalent production candidates and NOT two future product
implementations. Before a real release, reconcile onto one carefully selected
integration base with the approved Store + Mini behaviour and prove both again.
Never merge one onto the other by force or silently overwrite approved work.

Current Gate 1 **cannot** activate Community Saver or run real agreement execution.
It requires a visual owner review, narrow tests/build, real API design/verification
and deployment validation. Production securepay.ke and estate.ke remain untouched.
