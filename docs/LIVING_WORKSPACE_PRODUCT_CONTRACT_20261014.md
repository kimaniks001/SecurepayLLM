# Living SecurePay Workspace — implementation contract (14 October 2026)

## Product intent
One workspace with left contextual tools, centre KS001 conversation and right living result plus SecurePay Helps. Desktop supports three coordinated regions; narrow/mobile screens offer Chat | My Work | Vision, with Circle reached from Community and tools in sheets. No mode creates commitments without user or authorised participant action.

## Modes and economic boundary
- **My Work / Agreement**: short, precise KS001; one material next question; free preparation, correction, relevant source understanding, review and formation. Existing Agreement and Money execution retain governance and disclosed transactional fees.
- **Vision**: individual dreaming; interested, exploratory KS001; gentle pacing; drafts are not Agreements. Generous free exploration, background metering of expensive intelligence, then optional purchase or cooldown. Saved data and core My Work remain readable/usable during cooldown; no surprise charge.
- **Circle**: shared dreaming with tools, conversations, collaborative Vision, proposals and governed decisions. Circle allowance independent from each person's allowance. Members' ideas are not collective decisions; Circle membership/access separate from Trust Project membership.
- **Community**: belonging, LIVE, discoverable Circles, events, opportunities, Projects, Skills Institute and verified impact. A first-class destination, not only recommendations in the right column.

## Tool system
First-party tools: Places, Documents, Photos, Vision sketch/notes, Stores, People/Plugs/Masters, Money, Financial Institutions, Milestones, Skills Institute, Circles/Community, Projects/Events. Future embedded tools enter via capability registration; declare input/output schema, user-visible price, permissions, consent, provenance and revocation. Tool outcomes can create **proposals** but cannot directly authorise Money, approve or change accepted Agreements.

## Three surfaces
1. Left: resting compact toolbox; KS001 may suggest and prepare relevant tool; user can open any tool. Do not steal typing/focus or interrupt a working user. Finish -> acknowledge -> retract; opt to stay open.
2. Centre: conversation with sources and tool actions; mode-appropriate pacing. Preserve scroll/drafts when switching tabs; conversation always available.
3. Right: living facts with source and certainty, current Agreement/Vision/Circle state, review/approval, milestones, financial readiness; below that SecurePay Helps with stores, people, community, possible Community Saver. Suggestions never mutate agreements.

## Material fact pipeline
Source/Place/file/photo -> interpreted candidate -> user confirmation or explicit established basis -> draft term -> proposed review -> accepted/authorised version. Track provenance, versions, permissions, date, actors, confidence and disagreements. On upload distinguish saved / parsed / proposed / confirmed / failed. For a photo allow linked chat caption. Place selection updates draft only; an accepted Agreement demands amendment workflow.

## SecurePay Helps
Offers should be explainable, relevant, dismissible and permission-safe. Stores, Circles, Plugs, Masters, learning, Projects, financial institutions and potential Community Saver appear only on suitable terms. Community Saver always requires Plug verification, coordination, consent and agreement adaptation; no private Agreement leakage. A Circle's private content never surfaces outside authorised audience.

## Milestones and Money
Simple Agreement can evolve into milestone-based arrangement without restarting. Milestones are proposed until authorised; each holds due date, deliverables, evidence, review and permitted release conditions. Money is a left tool and right financial state; quote/intent/version authority remain in backend, never AI or partner tools.

## Delivery slices and visual gates
- Slice 0: source audit and architecture/authority mapping, baseline snapshots.
- Slice 1: shell, responsive regions, mobile tabs/sheets, keyboard/focus behaviour.
- Slice 2: KS001 mode semantics + contextual tool launcher + Places/files/photos actions.
- Slice 3: living Agreement facts + review/approval + milestone growth + provenance.
- Slice 4: Vision living results + generous metering, cooldown/paid-choice states and work independence.
- Slice 5: Circle shared Vision + membership, governance, permissions and decisions.
- Slice 6: Stores, Money, Financial Institutions, SecurePay Helps, Community Saver and Community surfaces.
- Slice 7: visual polish (desktop, tablet, 390/360/320 mobile), accessibility, realistic first-time-human tests and production hardening.

Each slice must ship an inspectable browser preview, screenshots in multiple widths, keyboard/mobile audit, explicit authority checks and regression tests. No merge/deployment until human certification and user approval.

## Acceptance journeys
A. Person starts a chair-delivery Agreement, picks saved Place, uploads quotation, captions photo, updates draft, invites party and completes approval.
B. Contractor task progresses from simple contract to authorised milestones, verification and Money readiness.
C. Person dreams about a workshop without being forced into Agreement; gathers sources, stores and ideas, encounters free allowance cooldown, can still view saved Vision and use My Work.
D. Circle proposes shared workshop, members contribute, governed decision captured, approved material is selectively used in Agreement.
E. Community browse LIVE, Projects, Circles, Masters; KS001 surfaces explainable help without showing private conversations.
F. External paid tool clearly asks permission and shows price; decline safely preserves user state.

This is a design/implementation contract, not proof that runtime behaviour has been built or certified.
